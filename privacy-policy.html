1 Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details: name, mobile number, and email address (if you sign in with Google).
  • Profile information: profile photo and delivery addresses.
  • Business accounts: shop name, shop address and shop photos.
  • Delivery partners only: driving licence number, a photo of your driving licence, vehicle type and vehicle number. These are collected to verify that you are eligible to make deliveries and are used for no other purpose.
  • Listings and content: product images, land or second-hand item listings, descriptions, prices, and contact details you choose to publish.
  • Orders and activity: orders, delivery and return requests, referrals, rewards, and game activity.
  • Messages you start: when you tap a WhatsApp or call button, the app opens WhatsApp or your dialer with the number you selected. We do not read your WhatsApp conversations.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and notification data: a push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send you order and account updates.
  • Crash reports (only with your approval): if the app crashes, it asks whether you want to send a report the next time you open it. If you agree, we receive your user ID, account role, app version, Android version, device model, the screen you were on, and technical error details. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.
  • Location: see Section 3.
We do not collect your contacts, SMS messages, or call logs.

2 How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in securely.
  • To process orders, arrange deliveries and returns, and connect customers, businesses and delivery partners.
  • To show nearby shops, services and listings, and to calculate delivery routes.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To send order updates, account alerts and, where applicable, offers or reward notifications.
  • To operate rewards, referrals and games, including showing video ads that may earn you rewards.
  • To provide support, improve the app, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3 Location information

With your permission, the app uses your device’s precise and approximate location to:

  • find nearby stores and services and set delivery addresses;
  • show maps and calculate routes; and
  • for delivery partners, share live location with the customer and our operations while a delivery is active, so the order can be tracked and completed. This runs as a visible foreground notification while you are on a delivery.

Location is only collected after you grant permission. You can withdraw permission at any time in your device settings; some features (such as delivery tracking and nearby search) may then stop working.

  • Business transfer: if we merge with or are acquired by another business, your information may be transferred, with notice to you.

5 Payments

Online payments are handled by Cashfree Payments. Your card, UPI or banking details are entered on Cashfree’s secure checkout and are not stored on our servers. We receive only the payment status and transaction reference needed to confirm your order.

6 Notifications

The app asks permission to send notifications on Android 13 and above. We use them for order status, delivery updates, account messages and rewards. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

7 Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service. Order and payment records are retained for 5 years for accounting, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and legal compliance. When you delete your account, we remove or anonymise your personal data as described in Section 10.

8 Data security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on our backend. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your login details private.

9 Your rights and choices

You can:

  • view and update your profile information in the app;
  • withdraw location, notification or photo-access permissions in your device settings;
  • request a copy or correction of your personal data; and
  • request deletion of your account and data.

To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 13.

10 Account and data deletion

You can delete your account directly in the app from the Delete Account option in your profile or settings. You can also visit our account deletion page, or contact us and we will process your request.

When an account is deleted, we remove or anonymise your personal information, profile photo and listings. Order and payment records are retained for 5 years as described in Section 7.

11 Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
