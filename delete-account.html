Delete Your Account

Rithan and Friends · Android App · Last updated: 24 November 2026

You can request deletion of your Rithan and Friends account and associated data at any time. This page explains how to do it and what happens afterwards.

1 Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Rithan and Friends app and sign in.
  2. Open your Profile screen (available in every account type).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete My Account.
This action is permanent and cannot be undone. After deletion you will be signed out and taken back to the login screen.

2 Can’t access the app? Request by email

If you cannot sign in or have uninstalled the app, email us from your registered email address, or include your registered mobile number, with the subject “Delete My Account”:

  • Email: rithankumaran320@gmail.com

We may ask you to verify that the account belongs to you before processing the request. We complete deletion within 15 days of verifying your request.

3 What gets deleted

  • Your profile information (name, mobile number, email) and profile photo
  • Saved addresses
  • Order history and activity linked to your account
  • Your listings and uploaded images, rewards and referral data
  • Push notification tokens linked to your device
  • For delivery partners: driving licence number, licence photo, vehicle type and vehicle number

Business and delivery-partner accounts have their shop details, photos and partner profile removed in the same way.

If your Customer account is linked to a separate Player account, deleting your Customer account unlinks it while preserving your Player account; to delete your Player account as well, open the Player Profile screen or request deletion by email.

4 What may be kept

Order and payment records are retained for 5 years for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution and legal compliance, and are deleted after that period. Payment card or UPI details are handled by Cashfree and are not stored by us.
